# BELLWATER — design notes

*The sea left Bellwater when you were small. Today you heard a bell.*

A small side-scrolling metroidvania (Godot 4.7, GDScript, 320×180 pixel art).
Jam-theme inspiration only: **Revival**, **Ruins**, **Music Guide You**, **Revisited**.

## The hook

1. **The sea is gone and you call it back.** The tide is one global height across one
   continuous world. Ringing a bell sends the whole sea to that bell's mark.
2. **The sea brings colour.** Everything above the highest tide reached so far is bleached sepia.
   Everything the sea has touched stays in full colour, permanently. Progress is visible at a glance.
3. **The Well is a tide-driven elevator.** A shaft runs down the middle of the world. You float, so
   the tide level decides which pair of doors you can reach.

## Rules of the water

- Without the Brass Helm you float. You can't go below the surface, so anything under water is locked
  until you can dive — and anything you can float up to is reachable only with the right tide.
- With the Helm you can dive, on a breath meter. Bubble vents refill it. Now you can reach bells
  that are under water, which is how you *lower* the sea.
- Loose sand washes away the first time the sea touches it, opening new routes.
- Heavy sea doors won't open against the weight of the water: to pass one you must lower the tide first.
- Ringing a bell is also a checkpoint, so a rising tide can never drown a respawn point.

## Progression

| # | Tide | Where | Get | Opens |
|---|------|-------|-----|-------|
| 1 | L0 | The Flats, sea wall | Sea-Wall Bell | Float up to the Quay |
| 2 | L1 | Well → Ropewalk | **Reel** (line to iron rings) | Crane rings up to Warehouse Row |
| 3 | L1 | Salt House | Boss: **The Hermit** (crab wearing a bell) → Salt Bell | L2 |
| 4 | L2 | Well → Cellars → Cliff → Lantern Row | **Brass Helm** (dive) | Everything under water |
| 5 | L2 | Well, west door at the foot → Cisterns | Boss: **The Conger** → Cistern Bell | L3 |
| 6 | L3 | Market → Bay → Lighthouse high door (barred) | The door is barred from inside | The keeper's sea door, down on the flats |
| 7 | any | Dive to the foot of the Well | **Ebb Bell** | L0: the sea goes out, and the flats are alive now |
| 8 | L0 | The Long Drain → Flats → sea door → Lighthouse | **Bellows** (double jump), Keeper's Bell | L3 from inside; unbar the high door |
| 9 | L3 | Fish Stairs → the Roofs | Boss: **The Albatross** (bell round its neck) → Albatross Bell | L4 |
| 10 | L4 | Well → Terrace → Belfry | **Tuning Fork** (call any known tide from anywhere) | The way down |
| 11 | L0 | Foot of the Well, three locks → the Sump | Boss: **The Drinker** | The sea returns; it carries you up the Well |
| 12 | L5 | The Belfry | Ring the Great Bell | Ending: the sky gets its colour back, and the town wakes |

Steps 5 and 6-8 can happen in either order: with the Helm you can reach the Ebb Bell before the Conger.
The Sump's locks only need the three bosses gone.

Optional: six Pearls (+1 health), an Air Sac (+breath), the Long Line (+reel range), the Barbed Hook (+damage).

## Technical shape

- Rooms are ASCII text files in `data/rooms/`, each placed on a global grid (a cell is 40×23 tiles).
  One continuous tile grid, so one water height applies everywhere.
- Custom pixel-exact AABB collision, with a central deterministic `tick()` — the game can be stepped
  headless, faster than real time, with scripted input.
- `tests/` contains a physics-accurate route planner that proves the game can be finished, using the
  real player code.
- All art and audio is generated by `tools/` (original work, no third-party assets).
